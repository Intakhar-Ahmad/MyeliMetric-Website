The MyeliMetric paper

The peer-reviewed article describes the rationale for a standardized, biologically informed g-ratio workflow and validates the original MyeliMetric approach with synthetic and published experimental datasets.

Open-access research article

MyeliMetric: A Python-Based Toolbox for Standardized G-Ratio Analysis of Axon-Myelin Integrity

Authors: Intakhar Ahmad, Farjana Sultana Chowdhury, Anne I. Boullerne, Alexander Gow, and Douglas L. Feinstein.

The article emphasizes standardized preprocessing, caliber-aware g-ratio analysis, reduction of common analytical artifacts, and reproducibility across experiments and laboratories.

Recommended citation

Ahmad I, Chowdhury FS, Boullerne AI, Gow A, Feinstein DL. MyeliMetric: A Python-Based Toolbox for Standardized G-Ratio Analysis of Axon-Myelin Integrity. ASN Neuro. 2026;18(1):2603411. doi:10.1080/17590914.2025.2603411.
